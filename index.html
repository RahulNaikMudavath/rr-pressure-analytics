1. Defining Delivery-Level Pressure (PI ∈ [1, 10])

Pressure is formulated as a dynamic, continuous leverage index rather than an arbitrary binary split. Every delivery receives a Pressure Index score based on three dimensional inputs:

• Chasing Innings (Innings 2):
Governed by Required Run Rate strain ((RRR - 5.5) / 2.0), Wicket Resource Depletion ((10 - W) / 10 × 2.5), and Endgame Crunch Criticality in death overs (≤ 36 balls remaining).
• Target-Setting Innings (Innings 1):
Governed by Early Top-Order Crisis/Collapse (≥2-3 wickets in powerplay, ≥4 wickets before over 12) and Death Overs Execution pressure (overs 16-20).
• Stakes & Quality Multipliers:
Knockouts/Playoffs (1.20× - 1.25×), Super Overs (PI = 10.0), and IPL/Elite International tier weighting.

2. Performance Measurement & Bayesian Shrinkage

Raw statistics deceive due to small-sample variance. Our framework solves this via a three-tier scoring engine:

A. Absolute Pressure Performance (70%):
Multi-factor scoring against population pressure baselines (Batting: SR, Avg, Boundary%, Dot Resistance; Bowling: Economy efficiency, Average efficiency, Dot%, Boundary suppression).
B. Clutch Delta Score (30%):
Measures whether the player elevates (ΔSR > 0, ΔEcon < +1.71 peer surge) or wilts under pressure compared to their own baseline.
C. Bayesian Credibility Shrinkage:
w = N / (N + N0) (N0 = 120 balls). Pulls small sample sizes towards the league mean, ensuring proven pedigree triumphs over flukes.
